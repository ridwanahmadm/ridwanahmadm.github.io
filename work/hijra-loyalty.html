Hijra loyalty levels

An exploration of four journey levels, Hijrah, Hamasah, Istiqomah and Qudwah, that make progress in ethical banking visible.

My part: The UI and interaction design of the concept.

Role
UI and interaction design
Type
Exploration
Focus
Progress and motivation
Platform
Mobile app

Four levels, one journey

Each level explains a stage of progress in ethical banking, from Hijrah to Qudwah.

Always show the next step

The level screen separates completed milestones, the current stage and the next goal. A progress bar gives each achievement context instead of a standalone badge.

Celebrate, then point forward

An achievement screen marks a new level and shows the way to the next goal, in encouraging language rooted in the product's values.

Scope

This is an interface exploration. No research or launch evidence exists, so no business impact is claimed.
