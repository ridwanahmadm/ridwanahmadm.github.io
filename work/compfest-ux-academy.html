Judging the COMPFEST 16 UX Academy

I judged the User Experience Academy at COMPFEST 16, Universitas Indonesia's annual technology festival, evaluating five teams of emerging designers.

The event

COMPFEST is organised every year by students of the Faculty of Computer Science at Universitas Indonesia. The 2024 User Experience Academy ran from 2 to 18 August with five teams of five participants.

My role

I evaluated the teams' work with a product lens, looking for clear problem framing, evidence and decisions, not only polish. This entry records the contribution; it does not claim a measured learning outcome.
